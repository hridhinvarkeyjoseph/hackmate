# Two-minute demo

Before you start, clear the site data so the sample season loads, and keep the app open on **Overview**.

| Time | Do this | Say this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Show **Overview** | "Hackathons are scattered across sites, and students miss deadlines or travel for the wrong ones." |
| 0:20 | Open **Near me** and pick your city | "Hackmate only shows events you can still join." |
| 0:35 | Point at a Go and a Skip, then use the filters | "Each verdict weighs distance, your interests and your calendar, and tells you why." |
| 0:55 | Press **Track it** on a Go | "One tap puts it on my board." |
| 1:05 | Press **Plan my sprint** | "It fits a full plan into the days I really have." |
| 1:20 | Tick tasks and watch **Submission readiness** rise | "I always know what to do next." |
| 1:35 | Open **Your data** and press **Create** under portfolio | "What I ship becomes a page for my resume." |
| 1:45 | Show the phone app or a narrow window | "It works offline and on Android, with no account, and my data stays with me." |

Have a backup: record this flow once, in case the venue's internet or projector fails.
