# Two-minute demo

Before you start, open the app in a private window so the welcome screen appears.

| Time | Do this | Say this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Show the welcome screen | "Hackathons are scattered across sites, and students miss deadlines or travel for the wrong ones." |
| 0:20 | Pick a city and interests, then press **Show my hackathons** | "In seconds, Hackmate shows only events I can still join." |
| 0:35 | Point at a Go and a Skip, then use the filters | "Each verdict weighs distance, your interests and your calendar, and tells you why." |
| 0:55 | Press **Track it** on a Go | "One tap puts it on my board." |
| 1:05 | Press **Plan my sprint** | "It fits a full plan into the days I really have." |
| 1:20 | Tick tasks and watch **Submission readiness** rise | "I always know what to do next." |
| 1:30 | Press **Share plan with my team** and open the link in another window | "My team joins with one link and no accounts." |
| 1:40 | Open **Your data** and press **Create** under portfolio | "What I ship becomes a page for my resume." |
| 1:50 | Show the phone app or a narrow window | "It works offline and on Android, with no account, and my data stays with me." |

Have a backup: record this flow once, in case the venue's internet or projector fails.
